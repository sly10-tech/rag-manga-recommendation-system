# Master Index: Action, Fantasy, Murim, and System Series

- Solo Leveling: Action, Super Power, Fantasy, System, Power Leveling. Sung Jinwoo gains a unique leveling system, transforming into an unstoppable shadow monarch.
- Tower of God: Action, Adventure, Fantasy, Super Power, Mystery. Bam climbs a massive tower filled with deadly trials to reunite with his friend Rachel.
- The Beginning After the End: Action, Fantasy, Magic, Reincarnation, Isekai. A mighty king reincarnates into a world of magic and monsters, retaining his martial intellect.
- Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint: Action, Apocalyptic, System, Supernatural, Meta. Kim Dokja finds himself living inside the plot of his favorite apocalyptic web novel.
- Return of the Mount Hua Sect: Action, Murim, Martial Arts, Reincarnation, Comedy. Chung Myung reincarnates 100 years into the future to revive his fallen sword sect.
- The Greatest Estate Developer: Action, Comedy, Fantasy, System, Building, Isekai. Lloyd civil-engineers his way out of debt in a fantasy world using absurd engineering hacks.
- Murim Login: Action, Murim, System, Comedy, Martial Arts. A weak hunter connects to a VR game that transports him into a real, cutthroat martial arts world.
- Nano Machine: Action, Sci-Fi, Murim, System, Revenge. An illegitimate prince receives a future nano-machine injection that turns him into a godlike martial artist.
- Legend of the Northern Blade: Action, Martial Arts, Murim, Revenge, Swordsmanship. Jin Mu-Won trains in secret to avenge his fallen sect and clean the corrupted Murim world.
- Jujutsu Kaisen: Action, Supernatural, School Life, Shounen, Dark Fantasy. Yuji Itadori swallows a cursed finger to save friends, joining sorcerers to defeat deadly curses.
- Chainsaw Man: Action, Dark Fantasy, Supernatural, Devil, Gore. Denji merges with his pet devil Pochita to become Chainsaw Man, hunting devil threats for the Public Safety Division.
- Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba: Action, Supernatural, Historical, Shounen. Tanjiro Kamado sets out to become a demon slayer to cure his turned sister Nezuko.
- Pick Me Up, Infinite Gacha: Action, System, Gaming, Survival, Strategy. A top-ranked gamer is summoned as a 1-star hero in a brutal gacha game and must lead troops to survive.
- SSS-Class Suicide Hunter: Action, System, Regression, Fantasy, Tower. Confucius gains a skill that lets him regress upon death, taking down tower bosses through tragedy and empathy.
- Overgeared: Action, Gaming, VRMMO, System, Comedy. Grid stumbles upon a legendary blacksmith class in a virtual game, crafting broken god-tier equipment.
- Latna Saga: Survival Story of a Sword King: Action, Fantasy, Isekai, Comedy. Hanbin is trapped in a glitched tutorial zone for decades, emerging with absurd strength and a bugged UI.
- Hero Killer: Action, Super Power, Revenge, Anti-Hero. Ehwa hunts down corrupt superheroes in a dystopian world to uncover her sister's murder.
- The Boxer: Action, Sports, Boxing, Drama, Psychological. An emotionless genius boxer dismantles opponents under the guidance of a legendary coach.
- Eleceed: Action, Super Power, Comedy, Cats, Mentorship. Jiwoo, a kind teenager with super speed, teams up with Kayden, a secret high-tier awakened user stuck in a fat cat's body.
- Mercenary Enrollment: Action, School Life, Bodyguard, Military. Yu Ijin, a former child mercenary, returns home to protect his sister while attending high school.
- Study Group: Action, School Life, Martial Arts, Comedy. Gamin Yoon wants nothing more than to study hard, but must fight his way through a school full of delinquents to do so.
- God of High School: Action, Martial Arts, Supernatural, Gods. Mori Jin competes in an all-out martial arts tournament that escalates into battles between gods and mythic beings.

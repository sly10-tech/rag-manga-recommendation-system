# Master Index: Romance, Otome Isekai, and Drama Series

- Villains Are Destined to Die: Reincarnation, Transmigration, Otome Isekai, Villainess, Drama. Penelope transmigrates as the hard-mode villainess in a dating sim where one mistake means death.
- The Remarried Empress: Romance, Drama, Fantasy, Revenge, Royalty, Webtoon. Empress Navier's husband divorces her for a mistress, leading her to marry the rival emperor Heinrey.
- Kill the Villainess: Transmigration, Villainess, Tragedy, Dark Fantasy, Isekai. Eris wakes up as a universally hated villainess and desperately tries to escape the story's tragic fate.
- Beware the Villainess!: Isekai, Villainess, Comedy, Romance, Parody. Melissa transmigrates as a villainess and hilariously shuts down all toxic male lead archetypes.
- Who Made Me a Princess: Reincarnation, Magic, Fantasy, Royalty, Family. Athanasia reincarnates as a doomed princess and must charm her cold-blooded emperor father to survive.
- Roxana: Transmigration, Dark Fantasy, Villainess, Revenge, Psychopath. Roxana uses poisonous butterflies and psychological tactics to protect her brother in a family of villains.
- My In-Laws Are Obsessed With Me: Romance, Drama, Mystery, Transmigration, Family. Pereshati enters a contractual marriage with a cursed grand duke, gaining an obsessively supportive family.
- Secret Lady: Romance, Fantasy, Mystery, Ghosts, Transmigration. Rozentin can see ghosts and uses her secret abilities to save the imperial prince from assassination attempts.
- Kaguya-sama: Love Is War: Romance, Comedy, School Life, Psychological. Two genius student council leaders engage in complex mental warfare to force the other to confess first.
- Fruits Basket: Romance, Shoujo, Supernatural, School Life, Drama. Tohru Honda lives with the Sohma family, who are cursed to turn into Zodiac animals when hugged by the opposite sex.
- Horimiya: Romance, School Life, Slice of Life, Comedy. Hori and Miyamura discover each other's secret personalities outside of school and fall in love.
- True Beauty: Romance, School Life, Drama, Webtoons, Makeup. Jugyeong uses makeup to transform into a high school goddess while navigating a love triangle.
- Clannad: Romance, Drama, School Life, Slice of Life, Visual Novel. High schooler Tomoya forms deep emotional bonds while helping classmates navigate life's heartbreaks.
- Lore Olympus: Romance, Supernatural, Webtoons, Drama, Greek Mythology. A modern retelling of the romance between Persephone and Hades in a colorful art style.
- See You in My 19th Life: Romance, Reincarnation, Drama, Webtoons. Ban Ji-eun remembers all her past lives and uses her 19th life to reconnect with her childhood love.
- A Business Proposal: Romance, Comedy, Workplace, Fake Dating. Shin Ha-ri goes on a blind date in place of her friend, only to discover the date is her company CEO.

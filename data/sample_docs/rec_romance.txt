# Master Index: Realistic, Modern, and Slice-of-Life Romance Series

## Workplace & Adult Modern Romance
- Wotakoi: Love is Hard for Otaku: Romance, Slice of Life, Workplace, Comedy. A secret closet otaku office worker reconnects with her childhood gamer friend at her new job, leading to a hilarious adult relationship.
- A Business Proposal: Romance, Comedy, Workplace, Fake Dating, Webtoon. Ha-ri goes on a blind date in her friend's place to scare off the guy, only to discover he is her company's young CEO.
- I Have a Crush at Work: Romance, Workplace, Slice of Life, Adult. Two office colleagues fall in love and try to keep their sweet relationship a secret from their coworkers.
- Positively Yours: Romance, Drama, Workplace, Unexpected Pregnancy, Webtoon. A single teacher has an accidental one-night stand that leads to a surprising, wholesome contract marriage with a handsome CEO.
- Age Matters: Romance, Workplace, Drama, Webtoon. A 29-year-old woman taking a break from love accidentally becomes the personal assistant to a young, aloof tech CEO.

## School Life, High School & Wholesome Rom-Coms
- Horimiya: Romance, School Life, Slice of Life, Comedy. Hori, a popular high school girl, and Miyamura, a quiet gloomy boy, discover each other's hidden personalities outside of school and fall in love.
- Kaguya-sama: Love Is War: Romance, Comedy, School Life, Psychological. Two genius high school student council leaders engage in complex mental warfare to force the other to confess their feelings first.
- Honey Lemon Soda: Romance, School Life, Shoujo, Drama. A shy, introverted high school girl meets a popular lemon-haired boy who helps her break out of her shell and experience youth.
- A Condition Called Love: Romance, School Life, Shoujo, Wholesome. A girl who has never understood romantic love gives a chance to a classmate who falls deeply for her after a small act of kindness.
- Seasons of Blossom: Romance, School Life, Drama, Slice of Life, Webtoon. Follows high school students navigating the sweetness of first loves, friendship, heartbreaks, and seasonal youth.
- Our Beloved Summer: Romance, School Life, Drama, Reunion, Webtoon. High school exes who swore never to see each other again are forced back together years later to film a follow-up documentary.
- Daytime Star: Romance, Slice of Life, Drama, Celebrity, Webtoon. An underrated minor actress meets a top movie star on set, starting a sweet and supportive adult romance.

## College & Youth Slice-of-Life Romance
- Cheese in the Trap: Romance, Psychological, College, Drama, Webtoon. A hard-working university student navigates a subtle, mysterious relationship with a deceptively perfect senior.
- Something About Us: Romance, College, Friends to Lovers, Slice of Life, Webtoon. Two longtime best friends in college slowly realize their comfortable friendship is turning into romantic love.
- Maybe Meant to Be: Romance, Comedy, Slice of Life, Marriage, Webtoon. Two unemployed childhood friends impulsively decide to get married to stop their parents from nagging them.

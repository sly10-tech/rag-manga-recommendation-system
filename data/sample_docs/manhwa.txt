# Manhwa

Manhwa is the general Korean term for comics and print cartoons. Outside Korea, the term usually refers to South Korean comics. Manhwa has been heavily influenced by Japanese manga, but has developed its own distinct style and format, particularly with the rise of digital webtoons.

Unlike traditional print comics, modern manhwa is frequently published digitally as webtoons, designed to be read by scrolling vertically on smartphones. Popular manhwa genres include action, fantasy, litRPG (system-based power leveling), romance fantasy, and drama. Notable examples of popular manhwa include Solo Leveling, Tower of God, The Beginning After the End, and Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint.

# Tagged Index: Reincarnation, Manhua, Martial Arts, Isekai, Magic, Shoujo & Survival

## Manhua, Cultivation & Martial Arts (Supernatural / Super Power / Comedy)
- Magic Emperor (Demonic Emperor): Manhua, Reincarnation, Action, Martial Arts, Magic, Supernatural, Strategy, Comedy. A ruthless demonic emperor is betrayed and reincarnates into the body of a powerless family servant, using past knowledge to protect his new master.
- Ultimate of All Ages (Eternal Supreme): Manhua, Reincarnation, Martial Arts, Super Power, Magic, Comedy, Action. Former martial sovereign Gu Feiyang reincarnates 15 years later into a weak young noble, defying divine fate through martial prowess.
- Top Tier Providence: Secretly Cultivate for a Thousand Years: Manhua, Transmigration, Comedy, Isekai, Martial Arts, Supernatural. Han Jue transmigrates into a cultivation world and chooses to quietly train in secret to reach immortality without drawing unwanted attention.
- Tales of Demons and Gods: Manhua, Reincarnation, Time Travel, Martial Arts, Magic, School Life, Adventure. Nie Li dies fighting a demon god and wakes up as his 13-year-old self, using future magic and martial arts knowledge to save his city and school.

## Reincarnation, School Life & Super Power (Action / Fantasy / Tragedy)
- Infinite Mage: Reincarnation, Magic, School Life, Fantasy, Super Power, Tragedy. A boy born to commoners discovers an incredible gift for spatial magic, attending an elite magic academy where he defies class prejudice and dark secrets.
- Warrior High School – Dungeon Raid Department: School Life, Super Power, Action, Fantasy, Martial Arts, Survival. In a world where dungeons open inside modern cities, a underground fighter enrolls in a prestigious raid academy to save his sick father.
- UnOrdinary: School Life, Super Power, Action, Drama, Tragedy, Supernatural. John attends a high school where society is strictly ranked by super powers, pretending to be a powerless low-tier while hiding a dangerous secret power.
- Talent-Swallowing Magician: Magic, School Life, Super Power, Fantasy, Action. Elric, a noble born with a blocked mana circuit, gains a cursed ability to absorb the talents and magic of rival mages.

## Shoujo, Transmigration, Tragedy & Romance (Fantasy / Magic)
- The Siren: Becoming the Villain's Family: Shoujo, Reincarnation, Tragedy, Magic, Fantasy, Romance, Revenge. Aria, a siren whose voice can control minds, dies in a cage before regressing to childhood to save the young grand duke who saved her.
- Father, I Don't Want This Marriage!: Shoujo, Transmigration, Comedy, Romance, Magic, Fantasy. Juvelian reincarnates as a doomed villainess in a novel and pretends to be helpless to avoid a tragic execution by her strict father.
- Reminiscence Adonis: Shoujo, Reincarnation, Martial Arts, Swordsmanship, Magic, Fantasy, Tragedy. Ianna dies at the hands of her eternal rival Arhad, only to reincarnate with her memories intact, swearing to become his loyal knight instead.
- The Golden-Haired Summoner: Shoujo, Transmigration, Isekai, Magic, Comedy, Adventure. Jinny reincarnates into a noble family in a magic world, becoming a genius elemental summoner who just wants to live lazily.

## Survival, Tragedy, Supernatural & Isekai
- Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint: Survival, Apocalyptic, System, Isekai, Supernatural, Action, Tragedy, Super Power. Kim Dokja relies on his knowledge of a 3,000-chapter web novel to survive deadly apocalyptic scenarios imposed on Earth.
- Dungeon Odyssey: Survival, System, Fantasy, Adventure, Comedy, Super Power. Born inside a dangerous underground dungeon, Kim Jinwoo manages a labyrinth territory while fighting off subterranean monsters and rival lords.
- SSS-Class Suicide Hunter: Survival, Reincarnation, Tragedy, System, Fantasy, Super Power. Confucius gains the ability to copy a skill upon dying and going back 24 hours, using his loops to empathize with tragically fallen tower bosses.

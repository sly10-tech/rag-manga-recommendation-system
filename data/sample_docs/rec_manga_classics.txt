# Master Index: Iconic & Best-Selling Manga Series

## Battle Shounen, Adventure & Fantasy
- One Piece: Action, Adventure, Fantasy, Shounen, Comedy. Monkey D. Luffy and his Straw Hat crew sail the seas to find the legendary treasure One Piece and become King of the Pirates.
- Dragon Ball: Action, Martial Arts, Fantasy, Shounen, Super Power. Goku embarks on a journey searching for the seven Dragon Balls while battling powerful martial artists and extraterrestrial threats.
- Naruto: Action, Ninja, Shounen, Super Power, Fantasy. Uzumaki Naruto, a young ninja shunned by his village, strives to earn respect and achieve his dream of becoming Hokage.
- Bleach: Action, Supernatural, Shounen, Swordsmanship, Super Power. Ichigo Kurosaki gains the powers of a Soul Reaper and must protect humans and spirits from malicious Hollows.
- Hunter x Hunter: Action, Adventure, Fantasy, Super Power, Shounen. Gon Freecss discovers his father is a famous Hunter and takes the rigorous Hunter Exam to find him.
- Attack on Titan: Dark Fantasy, Action, Mystery, Survival, Drama. Humanity fights for survival inside giant walled cities against maneating humanoids called Titans.
- Fullmetal Alchemist: Fantasy, Action, Magic, Steampunk, Drama. Brothers Edward and Alphonse Elric use alchemy to search for the Philosopher's Stone to restore their damaged bodies.
- JoJo's Bizarre Adventure: Action, Supernatural, Super Power, Adventure, Mystery. Follows generations of the Joestar family using unique spiritual powers (Stands) to defeat evil threats.

## Seinen, Dark Fantasy & Sports
- Vagabond: Historical, Martial Arts, Samurai, Seinen, Philosophy. A fictionalized story detailing the life of legendary Japanese swordsman Miyamoto Musashi as he seeks true strength.
- Vinland Saga: Historical, Action, Military, Seinen, Revenge. Thorfinn joins the mercenary band of his father's killer to challenge him to a duel, becoming embroiled in historical wars.
- Kingdom: Historical, War, Action, Military, Seinen. Set during China's Warring States period, orphan Xin fights on the battlefield to become the greatest general under the heavens.
- Real: Sports, Wheelchair Basketball, Drama, Seinen, Slice of Life. Three young men deal with physical disabilities and personal struggles through wheelchair basketball and youth growth.
- Slam Dunk: Sports, Basketball, School Life, Shounen, Comedy. Hanamichi Sakuragi joins his school's basketball team to impress a crush, growing into a passionate athlete.
- Detective Conan (Case Closed): Mystery, Crime, Shounen, Detective, Comedy. High school detective Shinichi Kudo is turned into a child by a secret poison and solves crimes while seeking a cure.

# Master Index: Top Visual Novels & Psychological Narratives

## Psychological Horror, Meta & Choice-Driven
- Slay the Princess: Psychological Horror, Choice-Driven, Cosmic, Comedy, Meta. You are tasked with slaying a princess in a cabin; doing so or refusing leads to mind-bending, shifting realities.
- Tsukihime: A Piece of Blue Glass Moon: Urban Fantasy, Vampire, Supernatural, Mystery, Visual Novel. Shiki Tohno can see lines of destruction on objects and people, getting dragged into a dark supernatural world.
- Fate/stay night: Urban Fantasy, Action, Magic, Battle Royale, Romance. High schooler Shirou Emiya accidentally summons the servant Saber to participate in the deadly Holy Grail War.
- Ghost Trick: Phantom Detective: Mystery, Supernatural, Time Manipulation, Puzzle, Visual Novel. Play as Sissel, a spirit with amnesia who uses supernatural powers to manipulate objects and alter past deaths.

## Crime, Mystery & Drama
- Bustafellows: Crime, Mystery, Romance, Sci-Fi, Visual Novel. Teuta, a journalist with the ability to rewind time into other people's bodies, works alongside legal vigilantes in a crime city.
- Danganronpa: Trigger Happy Havoc: Murder Mystery, Psychological, School Life, Survival, Visual Novel. Ultimate students trapped in Hope's Peak Academy are forced into court trials to solve murders committed by classmates.
- Master Detective Archives: RAIN CODE: Mystery, Detective, Supernatural, Urban, Visual Novel. Yuma, an amnesiac detective trainee, solves murder mysteries alongside a spirit companion in a rain-soaked megacity.

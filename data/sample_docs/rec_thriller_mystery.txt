# Master Index: Thriller, Horror, Mystery, Time Travel, and Survival Series

- Steins;Gate: Sci-Fi, Time Travel, Psychological Thriller, Visual Novel. Okabe Rintaro accidentally invents a time machine, triggering dark butterfly effects across world lines.
- Higurashi When They Cry: Psychological Horror, Mystery, Time Travel, Tragedy, Visual Novel. Keiichi moves to Hinamizawa, uncovering terrifying violent loops and local curses.
- The House in Fata Morgana: Gothic Horror, Mystery, Romance, Tragedy, Visual Novel. A spirit navigates a cursed mansion through different historical eras to uncover forgotten memories.
- Sweet Home: Survival, Horror, Monsters, Webtoons, Apocalyptic. A reclusive teenager must team up with apartment neighbors as humans turn into monsters reflecting their desires.
- Monster: Psychological Thriller, Mystery, Crime, Seinen. Dr. Kenzo Tenma hunts down Johan Liebert, a charismatic serial killer patient whose life he saved years ago.
- Tokyo Revengers: Time Travel, Action, Delinquents, Drama, Revenge. Takemichi travels back 12 years to his middle school days to save his ex-girlfriend from gang violence.
- Bastard: Psychological Thriller, Horror, Mystery, Webtoons, Crime. Jin Seon must protect the girl his serial-killer father has targeted as his next victim.
- Pigpen: Psychological Thriller, Horror, Mystery, Amnesia, Twist. A young man wakes up on an isolated island with amnesia, living with an unsettling family hiding dark secrets.
- Gantz: Sci-Fi, Action, Horror, Survival, Gore. Resurrected dead people are forced to participate in a brutal alien-hunting game in modern Tokyo.
- 999: Nine Hours, Nine Persons, Nine Doors: Time Travel, Escape Room, Sci-Fi, Mystery, Visual Novel. Nine people are trapped on a sinking vessel and forced to solve fatal puzzles.
- Danganronpa: Murder Mystery, Psychological, School Life, Survival, Visual Novel. High school prodigies are locked in a school and forced into a killing game where they must solve court trials.
- Umineko When They Cry: Murder Mystery, Fantasy, Logic Puzzle, Visual Novel. A wealthy family is trapped on an island during a typhoon as a witch begins a bloody game.

# Comprehensive Recommendations Engine: Manga, Manhwa, Webtoons & Visual Novels

## Action, Super Power & System (Manhwa / Webtoons / Manga)
- **Solo Leveling**: Action, Super Power, Fantasy, System, Power Leveling. Sung Jinwoo gains a unique leveling system, transforming from the weakest hunter into an unstoppable shadow monarch.
- **Tower of God**: Action, Adventure, Fantasy, Super Power, Mystery. Twenty-Fifth Bam climbs a massive tower filled with deadly trials to reunite with his friend Rachel.
- **The Beginning After the End**: Action, Fantasy, Magic, Reincarnation, Isekai. A mighty king reincarnates into a world of magic and monsters, retaining all his former memories and martial intellect.
- **Omniscient Reader's Viewpoint**: Action, Apocalyptic, System, Supernatural, Meta. Kim Dokja finds himself living inside the plot of his favorite apocalyptic web novel.
- **Jujutsu Kaisen**: Action, Supernatural, School Life, Shounen, Dark Fantasy. Yuji Itadori swallows a cursed finger to save friends, joining jujutsu sorcerers to defeat deadly curses.
- **Chainsaw Man**: Action, Dark Fantasy, Supernatural, Devil, Gore. Denji merges with his pet devil Pochita to become Chainsaw Man, hunting devil threats for the Public Safety Division.
- **Demon Slayer: Kimetsu no Yaiba**: Action, Supernatural, Historical, Shounen. Tanjiro Kamado sets out to become a demon slayer to cure his turned sister Nezuko and avenge his family.
- **Eleceed**: Action, Super Power, Comedy, Cats, Mentorship. Jiwoo, a kind teenager with super speed, teams up with Kayden, a secret high-tier awakened user stuck in a fat cat's body.

# Master Index: Sports, Comedy, Gaming, and Mecha Series

- Blue Lock: Sports, Action, Psychological, Competition. Three hundred high school strikers are locked in a battle-royale facility to create Japan's ultimate selfish striker.
- Haikyu!!: Sports, School Life, Teamwork, Drama. Hinata Shouyou joins Karasuno High's volleyball team to prove that height isn't everything in sports.
- Wind Breaker: Sports, Action, School Life, Cycling, Delinquents. Jay Jo, a top student, gets pulled into street bicycle racing with his school's cycling crew.
- Slam Dunk: Sports, School Life, Comedy, Basketball. Delinquent Hanamichi Sakuragi joins the basketball team to impress a girl, falling in love with the sport.
- Neon Genesis Evangelion: Mecha, Psychological, Sci-Fi, Survival, Drama. Teenagers pilot giant bio-mechanical Mechas to defend humanity against apocalyptic monsters called Angels.
- 86 -Eighty-Six-: Mecha, Sci-Fi, Military, Drama, Survival. Persecuted soldiers fight an automated drone war on the frontlines while controlled by a distant handler.
- Grand Blue Dreaming: Comedy, Slice of Life, College, Diving, Alcohol. Iori joins a college diving club that is actually an excuse for absurd drinking games and comedic antics.
- Gintama: Comedy, Action, Sci-Fi, Parody, Samurai. Gintoki Sakata works as a freelancer in an alien-occupied feudal Japan, offering hilarious pop-culture parodies.
